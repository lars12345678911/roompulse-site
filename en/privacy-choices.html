Privacy Choices

RoomPulse has no separate user account or developer-operated database. Most choices are therefore handled directly on the device or through Apple.

Delete local app data

  • Delete custom cards in the app if you only want to remove that content.
  • A checkpoint for an unfinished game is kept locally for up to seven days and is replaced or removed automatically. The checkpoint is excluded from device backups.
  • Custom cards and other standard app data may be included in Apple-managed device backups or device-to-device transfers, depending on the device settings.
  • Delete RoomPulse from your iPhone or iPad to remove all other locally stored app data from that device.

Manage result sharing

  • RoomPulse never shares results automatically. The share sheet opens only when you choose Share.
  • Player names are omitted by default. Include names only when the group wants this and the recipient is correct.
  • The selected app, service or person processes what you share under its own terms.

Purchases and payment information

  • RoomPulse does not receive card details. Apple processes the one-time in-app purchase.
  • Use “Restore purchases” in the app to recover a previous entitlement with the same Apple Account.
  • If you reinstall the app, the purchase entitlement can be restored through Apple.

No external RoomPulse account

  • RoomPulse has no developer-operated account or database containing player names, votes, scores or custom cards.
  • We therefore cannot retrieve, export or delete that local information on your behalf.
  • Purchase history managed by Apple is handled through the Apple Account and Apple privacy tools.

Privacy questions

Contact us if you need help understanding how RoomPulse handles information. Do not send passwords, payment details or gameplay content you do not want to share.
