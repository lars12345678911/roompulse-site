How to play

Four simple steps. No long rulebook.

A round takes only a few minutes, and the Room Reader role rotates automatically.

1

The Room Reader predicts

One player predicts which option the majority will choose.

2

Everyone votes privately

Everyone else receives the shared device in turn and votes in private.

3

The votes are revealed

See the percentages, whether the Reader was right, and names only when the group wants them.

4

Points are awarded

Special cards with double points, minority bonuses and unanimous jackpots keep the energy moving.
